Welcome to CoderDojo Banbridge

Make a game. Build a website. Bring a robot to life. Have fun with tech!

CoderDojo Banbridge is a completely free coding club for young people aged 7–17. Whether you’re trying coding for the first time or already have a project in mind, our volunteer mentors will help you explore your ideas and create something of your own.

Free · Ages 7–17 · All experience levels welcome

What could you create?

Design a game in Scratch, build a website, try Python or JavaScript, or experiment with micro:bits, Raspberry Pi computers and robots. There’s plenty to try, and you can choose projects that interest you.

Along the way, you can solve problems, share ideas and show others what you’ve made. All levels of coding experience are welcome!

Inspiring

Be part of something amazing

CoderDojo is a friendly place to be curious, try things out and learn alongside others. Our Dojo has been running in Banbridge for over 10 years! When you join CoderDojo Banbridge you become part of the global Code Club community - a network that has reached over 2 million young people across 100+ countries.

Research shows that 90% of young people who take part in Code Club activities increase their skills and independence in coding. They also develop confidence, creativity and critical thinking skills - things that they’ll benefit from through their lives!
